# Anti-AI-Slop Slide Design

AI presentation generators fail because they default to sterile symmetry, generic chrome, and repetitive templates. Use this checklist to purge every AI giveaway from any slide deck.

## 1. The AI Slide Blacklist (Remove Immediately)

### Color & Surface Clichés
- **Purple-to-electric-blue gradients**, neon-on-black glow cards, and generic cream-and-terracotta defaults.
- **Translucent glassmorphism KPI boxes** floating over blurry abstract blobs or 3D robot art.
- **Heavy drop shadows** around every rectangle or card.

### Micro-Clutter & Filler Copy
- **Dot + Pill Eyebrows above every title** (e.g., `● Q3 RESULTS`, `● MARKET ANALYSIS`).
- **Generic filler subtitles** that merely restate the title in longer words.
- **Emojis on every heading or bullet** (`🚀 Fast Growth`, `✨ Key Insight`, `💡 Idea`).
- **4-line legal/metadata footers** on every slide — keep a 1-line discreet footer or source citation *only* when citing data.

### Structural Clichés
- **Centered title + 3 identical icon-in-circle cards** repeated slide after slide.
- **Identical `01–08` boxed grids** where every box has equal visual weight ("no hierarchy, nothing stands out").
- **Split dark-sidebar "Contents" slides** with 8 tiny links.
- **Walls of bullets** (>3 bullets or >15 words per block).

## 2. Studio Replacements (What Graphic Designers Do Instead)

1. **One Core Takeaway Per Slide:** If a slide has 3 unrelated ideas, split it into 3 fast, high-impact slides.
2. **Insight-Driven Action Titles:** Replace topic labels (`"Market Size"`) with the conclusion (`"Mid-market adoption unlocks a $4B gap"`).
3. **Inline "Turn" Highlighting:** In a 2-line headline, highlight **only the key turning phrase** in your single accent color or a tight rounded pill background so skimmers grasp the message in 0.5 seconds.
4. **Single-Active-State Contrast:** In a 4-column timeline or option row, keep `N-1` items outlined/muted and fill **only 1** active item in solid brand accent.
5. **4-Step Opacity Ramps Instead of Gradients:** Derive secondary text, dividers, and subtle surfaces from `100% / 60% / 30% / 10%` opacity of your core dark or light base.
6. **Human/Tactile Restraint:** Cap text blocks at 1–2 short lines (`8–15` words). Let generous negative space (`35–55%`) do the framing.
