# Visual Storytelling & Brand-Consistent AI Playbook (v11.0)

Synthesized from Canva, Adobe, The Story Mill, Prezent.ai, Prezi, Clemson University (*The Art of Visual Storytelling*), DesignForce 101, TechView Africa, Design-Rise 2026, Skool, and Microsoft PowerPoint 2026.

---

## 1. Story-First Mapping (Before Opening Any Slide Tool)

Never open with *"About Us"*. Start at the **ending** (what you want the audience to feel, think, or do) and work backward:

1. **Audience & Objective (1 sentence):** Who is the hero (the customer/audience, NOT your company), and what single action should they take?
2. **Reject Ideas #1–#4 (Clemson Rule):** Discard your first 3–4 obvious cliché concepts (lightbulbs, handshakes, puzzle pieces, generic blue boxes) to find a fresh visual angle.
3. **The Story Spine:** *"Back in… Every day… Until one day… Because of that… Until finally…"*
4. **Select 3–7 Key Moments:** Cut every detour (shortest path A → E); every slide must advance the tension/resolution arc.
5. **Single Extended Metaphor:** Choose **one** concrete metaphor (e.g., *tangled wires -> single cable* for simplification, *bridge* for partnership, *staircase* for scale) and carry it coherently across the deck.
6. **Co-Creation ("Give Them a Picture Frame"):** Leave deliberate visual/mental room so the audience co-creates meaning rather than drowning in explanations.
7. **Hold Back the Logo:** Let the story land before stamping logos loudly across every slide.

## 2. Six-Decision One-Page Visual Brief (TechView Africa + Skool)

Lock these 6 decisions in a structured brief **before** generating any slides or images:

```yaml
PROJECT: <Deck / PDF title & format (16:9 PPTX | A4 Editorial PDF)>
AUDIENCE: <Exact role, decision style, viewing context (Live room / Async email / Mobile)>
PRIMARY_MESSAGE: <One sentence understood in <= 3 seconds>
DESIRED_ACTION: <Exact next step or decision>
BRAND_COLORS: <Primary Dark HEX, Light Surface HEX, Single Protagonist Accent HEX>
TYPOGRAPHY: <1 multi-weight family OR 2 harmonious fonts (e.g. Display + Body >=14pt)>
IMAGE_AND_ICON_STYLE: <Real photos / flat-lay / custom illustrations + 1 uniform icon style (e.g. 2px outline)>
CREATIVE_DIRECTION: <Mood keywords, visual metaphor, chapter color shifts, things to avoid>
```

**Preservation Rules:** Never alter, recolor, or distort an official brand logo, and never distort real human faces/identities.

## 3. Mood Levers, Color-Emotion Wheel & 12 Brand Archetypes

### 5 Mood Levers
- **Light:** Soft/warm = safe, optimistic; hard shadows = dramatic, urgent.
- **Color-Emotion Character Wheel (Clemson):**
  - *Red:* strength, urgency, passion | *Orange:* creativity, enthusiasm | *Yellow:* joy, intellect, energy | *Green:* safety, growth, sustainability | *Blue:* trust, stability, engineering rigor | *Purple:* luxury, ambition | *Black/Obsidian:* formality, elegance | *White/Bone:* clarity, openness.
- **Space:** Open/empty = calm, focus; dense = energy/urgency.
- **12 Jungian Archetypes (Tone Alignment):** Structure (*Ruler, Artist, Caregiver*), Spiritual (*Innocent, Sage, Explorer*), Impact (*Outlaw, Magician, Hero*), Connection (*Everyman, Jester, Lover*).

## 4. 10 High-Impact Visual Slide Archetypes (Prezent.ai + Microsoft 2026)

Rotate layouts deliberately based on content role:
1. **Concept Cover** (strong metaphor + bold 2-line headline + essential context only)
2. **SCQA / Executive Summary** (1-line scannable bullets, subtle dividers, async-ready)
3. **Hero Metric / Statement** (giant figure or 1-sentence punch, mathematically centered)
4. **Native Data Chart** (1 takeaway per chart, direct labels, neutral grays + 1 accent)
5. **Workflow / Process Diagram** (step-by-step interconnections, `1.5–2px` icons)
6. **Chronological Timeline** (staggered milestones or SmartArt-style nodes with time/phase tags)
7. **Side-by-Side Comparison** (before/after, problem/solution, split-screen contrast)
8. **Funnel / Hub-and-Spoke** (central emblem/product flanked symmetrically by 3–5 callouts)
9. **Case Study / Proof** (real photo + Challenge -> Solution -> Outcome)
10. **Action / Decision Closing** (clear next steps, concrete options, or thought-provoking Q&A)

## 5. Quality Gates: 3-Second Rule & Set-Level QA

- **3-Second Comprehension Test:** Can a viewer grasp the main message of a slide within **3 seconds**?
- **Set-Level QA (TechView Africa):** View **5 slides side-by-side** (Thumbnail View). Confirm they look like they belong to the exact same organization (consistent typography, palette, icon style, margins) while still varying layout rhythm!
- **Async Readiness:** Ensure titles and visual flow still make sense when read without a live speaker in email/Teams.
