# Industry Art Direction Matrix

Use this matrix in Step 0 to tailor colors, typography, imagery, and chart choices to the prompt's industry.

| Industry / Prompt Type | Primary Dark | Light Surface | Signal Accent | Secondary Support | Typography Pairing | Visual & Chart Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical / Industrial / Engineering** | `#0F172A` (Steel Navy) | `#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` | `#EA580C` (Signal Orange) | `#1D4ED8` (Cobalt) | `Montserrat` + `Calibri` (or `DM Sans`) | Native Bar/Column TCO charts, 2×2 capability grids with crisp icons, industrial site photos, clean comparison tables |
| **Executive / Finance / Board** | `#0B1325` (Midnight) | `#F6F5F1` (Warm Bone) | `#D97706` (Amber Gold) | `#059669` (Emerald) | `Montserrat` + `DM Sans` | Waterfall bridges, native financial bar charts, hairline comparison tables, SCQA executive summary |
| **Tech / SaaS / Startup Pitch** | `#111827` (Carbon) | `#FFFFFF` / `#F3F4F6` | `#2563EB` (Electric Blue) | `#10B981` (Mint) | `Montserrat` + `Poppins` | Product UI mockups/screenshots, growth line charts, asymmetric Bento metrics |
| **Hospitality / Restaurant / Club** | `#1E2923` (Deep Olive) | `#FAF5EB` (Warm Cream) | `#C2410C` (Terracotta) | `#B45309` (Warm Ochre) | `Georgia` (Display) + `DM Sans` | Full-bleed food/venue photography with dark scrims, warm 50/50 editorial splits |
| **Healthcare / Education** | `#134E4A` (Deep Teal) | `#FFFBF5` (Ivory) | `#E11D48` (Coral Red) | `#0284C7` (Sky Blue) | `Poppins` + `Calibri` | Clear step-by-step process cards, high-contrast diagrams, readable `16pt+` body |

| **PropTech / Modern Finance Tech** | `#291C4A` (Deep Plum) | `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) | `#8358FF` (Electric Violet) | `#10B981` (Mint) | `Montserrat` + `DM Sans` | Circular ring/donut gauges, vertical numbered node spines, 3-card portrait rows |
